Instantly know which bin. Zero campus guesswork.

In PCTVS cafeterias, STEM labs, and career shops, discarded materials often

end up contaminated in the wrong stream. EcoSort AI runs visual image

inference directly on your device to distinguish Paper , Plastic , and Cardboard

before you bin them.

The Challenge

Soiled food wrap, greasy paper trays, and

non-recyclable cups easily contaminate

whole recycling batches across both campus

wings.

The AI Assist

A custom-trained image model inspects

surface texture, shape, and labels to output

real-time confidence scores and handling

steps.

Class 0: Paper

DEMO: Paper bin

Clean Paper

Standard academic paper products without heavy

oil or wax laminations.

GENERAL EXAMPLES:

Notebook & looseleaf paper

Printed homework & handouts

Clean campus announcements & flyers

Reject: Greasy pizza boxes, waxed milk

cartons, or cafeteria food-wrapping paper.

Class 1: Plastic

DEMO: Plastic bin
Plastic Containers
General examples only. The model does not distinguish resin codes or verify local recyclability.
GENERAL EXAMPLES:

Clean water and soda bottles (empty)

Clear smoothie & juice cups (#1 PETE)

Rigid HDPE lab supply jugs (rinsed)

Reject: Plastic grocery bags, single-use

plastic straws, chip wrappers, or half-full

liquids.

Class 2: Cardboard

DEMO: Cardboard bin

Corrugated Cardboard

Thick shipping material, non-waxed boxes, and

sturdy paperboard backers.

GENERAL EXAMPLES:

Flattened delivery cartons

Stiff notebook chipboard backings

Clean equipment packaging boxes

Reject: Pizza boxes soaked with cheese

grease, or un-flattened boxes blocking bin

entry.
